# Staff training outline

Version **0.5.3**. Full detail: [COMPLETE_GUIDE.md](COMPLETE_GUIDE.md).

## Before training

- Staff belong to the **user group** (or role) selected in Configuration.
- Each requester has **exactly one** programme centre in capture scope.
- Search organisation units cover the centres they need to find or approve.
- Separate accounts for requester and approver.
- App shared in App Management so non-superusers can open it.

## Session flow (about 30–45 minutes)

1. **Administrator** — show Configuration: one active programme, identifier, display names, staff group. Validate and Save.
2. **Centre B (requester)** — Request Transfer, search by identifier, submit a non-clinical reason.
3. **Centre A (owner)** — open Messages (envelope) and Transfer Requests; approve or refuse.
4. **Centre B** — show Transferred / Refused status; after transfer, Open permitted history if allowed.
5. **Optional** — show a Needs review case and that blind retry is not used.

## Remind staff

- The title under Ownership Transfer is the **active programme name**, not the app name.
- Only **one** programme is active at a time; switch in Configuration if needed.
- Expanding search scope can increase access elsewhere in DHIS2 for OPEN programmes — follow local policy.
